# AI_AUDIT: catch and correct

Concrete cases where AI output was wrong or flawed, how it was detected, and what was changed.
Only real cases are listed. Each member adds their own.

## Case 1 (caught by Daniel FOSSO): wrong requirement stated by the AI
- AI output: the AI listed "a 2-4 page progress report in English" as part of the Week 2 deliverable.
- How it was caught: I asked "Why should i write a report ? [...] It isn't in the guideline ?" and re-read the professor's guidelines: Step 2 lists analyses, baseline, benchmark and AutoML v0.1, but no separate weekly report (only the final report).
- Correction: the AI confirmed the error; Week 2 is delivered as a self-explanatory notebook (Markdown cells with justifications), which will feed the final report.
